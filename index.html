Activation license, data protection & limitations

Manage your device activation key and understand what this standalone file protects.

Device activation & license status

Checking license…
Saved data is encrypted locally. Your assessment, snapshots, experiments, and roadmap are encrypted in browser storage with AES-GCM and a PBKDF2-derived key (310,000 iterations). Passphrases are never stored and no data leaves this device.

Safeguards included

  • ECDSA P-256 asymmetric license verification (client app contains only the public key).
  • No external scripts, fonts, analytics, or network calls.
  • Browser storage and exported JSON backups are AES-GCM encrypted.
  • Scores exclude unassessed dimensions and separately surface Evidence Verified %.
  • CSV action exports neutralize spreadsheet formula characters (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`).

Remaining limitations

  • No server-side license revocation or tamper-proof DRM in a standalone local HTML file.
  • Expiry checks rely on the local device clock (basic clock-rollback detection is applied).
  • Encryption protects data at rest, not while the vault is unlocked on a compromised device.
  • Losing your vault passphrase means encrypted data cannot be recovered. Keep separate backups.
